## About Me

I'm a Business Administration student (Marketing Management) building a career in data and business analysis. I like the part of analytics that most people skip: taking raw, messy data and turning it into something clean, checked, and useful for a decision.

My projects follow real workflows: clean the data, load it into a database, check it, and explain what it means for the business.
